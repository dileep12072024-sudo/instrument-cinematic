Fit into the model. The model hall is lower and shallower than these measurements, while its passage width and storey heights are tied to the plan and the exterior. Heights and sizes on the side walls are therefore scaled by S_V = 3.596 / 4.386 = 0.82. Positions along each wall are scaled by S_H = 3.45 / 4.90 = 0.704 from the passage edge, so boards keep their order and spacing (faces are about 14% narrower than true shape). Results: gate head 2.017, grille to 3.66, windows in `hallWindows` (`src/footprint.js`, shared by the hall walls and the shell openings), bench seat 0.53 and back top 0.86. A real correction would raise the ground storey and deepen the hall; that is a building-wide change and is not made here.
